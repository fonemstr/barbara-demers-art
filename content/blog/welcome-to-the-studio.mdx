Thank you for stopping by. This little corner of the internet is where I'll
share new paintings as they come out of the studio, keep a running archive
of what's for sale, and occasionally post a progress photo or a note from
the easel.

If you're looking for a specific animal — a pet, a wild subject, a working
horse — you can always commission one. The [commissions page](/commissions)
has the details.
